# Benchmark fonts — licence notes

The benchmark loads three Noto fonts (committed under `Assets/StreamingAssets/Fonts/`,
copied from the OpenGlyph package `Defaults/`):

- `NotoSans-Regular.ttf`
- `NotoSansArabic-Regular.ttf`
- `NotoSansHebrew-Regular.ttf`

  They may be bundled, embedded and redistributed; they may not be sold on their own.

These fonts are used here purely as neutral multi-script test material so the
benchmark exercises Latin, Arabic and Hebrew shaping on identical glyph sources
across OpenGlyph, TextMeshPro and UI Toolkit.
